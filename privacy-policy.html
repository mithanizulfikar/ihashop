Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Order details you provide at checkout: name, phone, address, email.
  • Messages you send us via the Contact page.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the store (e.g. what's in your cart, stored in your browser).

How we use it

Solely to process and deliver your order, respond to your messages, and improve the shop. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Data storage

Order and message data is stored securely in our database and kept only as long as needed to fulfil orders and comply with basic record-keeping.

Your rights

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time via the Contact page.

This is a starting-point policy — have it reviewed against your local data-protection laws before launch.
