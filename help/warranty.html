Warranty

Some of our categories come with extra coverage beyond the standard return window.

Jewelry

Manufacturing defects (e.g. a clasp failing under normal use) are covered for 90 days from delivery. Wear and tear from daily use is not covered.

Gadgets

Electronics are covered for defects for 30 days from delivery. If a gadget stops working under normal use in that window, contact us for a replacement or repair.

Household & Dresses

These are covered under our standard Return Policy rather than a separate warranty.

How to claim

Reach out via the Contact page with your order ID, a description of the issue, and a photo if possible.

This is a starting-point policy — adjust the coverage windows to match what you can realistically support.
