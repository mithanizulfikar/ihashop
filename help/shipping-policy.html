Shipping Policy

We aim to get every order packed and out the door quickly and safely.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of being placed. You'll be able to check progress anytime on the Track Order page.

Delivery time

Standard delivery typically takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. Remote areas may take a little longer.

Shipping costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your order size and delivery address.

Order tracking

Every order gets a unique ID (e.g. IHA-4F2A1B). Use it on the Track Order page to see the current status at any time.

This is a starting-point policy — edit it to match your actual courier, rates, and timelines before launch.
